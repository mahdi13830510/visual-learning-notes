# Rubric

Each criterion is scored 0–2 (0 = absent or wrong, 1 = partly, 2 = fully). Score the **rendered pages and the blueprint**, not the HTML source. The maximum is 32. Notes below 26, or with a 0 on criteria 4, 5 or 9, are not shippable.

| # | Criterion | 2 means |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Central problem** | stated in 1–2 lines at the top; the reader knows what is being solved and why it's hard |
| 2 | **Intuition before abstraction** | each key equation has its picture or plain meaning next to it, and the picture comes first |
| 3 | **Diagrams communicate** | every diagram passes the five-second test ("this shows that ___") |
| 4 | **Correct math** | signs, indices, dimensions and weights are right; every shown number is verified |
| 5 | **Accurate intuition** | no appealing-but-false simplifications; limitations are labelled |
| 6 | **Derivations kept** | intermediate steps that carry ideas are present, with margin reasons |
| 7 | **Relationships** | at least one cause → effect chain, comparison, or before/after where the topic has one |
| 8 | **Real example** | small, checkable by hand, and it hits the actual difficulty |
| 9 | **Assumptions & failure modes** | the conditions the result needs, and what breaks without them, are on the page |
| 10 | **Economy** | nothing removable; no paragraphs, no repeated summaries, no trivial symbol glossaries |
| 11 | **Handwriting-ready** | each page copies in ≤ ~30 min; diagrams can be drawn freehand; the blueprint gives sizes, positions, and exact labels |
| 12 | **Blueprint complete** | title, layout, draw, write, annotate, must-not-omit, and copy time per page; then quiz and Remember |
| 13 | **Looks human-made** | no cards, icons, emoji, banner headings, or symmetric box grids; asymmetric and with whitespace |
| 14 | **Colour has meaning** | blue/red/green/orange used consistently; survives grayscale |
| 15 | **Clean render** | no overlaps, overflow, KaTeX errors, or cut-off content; the PDF page count matches |
| 16 | **Understanding questions** | 3–5 why / what-if / compare / rebuild questions, no recall trivia |

## Mode A extra checks (lecture material)

- The source's notation is kept.
- Additions are marked **+** and nothing is attributed to the lecturer that the material doesn't contain.
- Slide structure is compressed into ideas, not transcribed bullet by bullet.
